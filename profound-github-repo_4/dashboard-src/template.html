Burn multiple reads low, and it is worth knowing why. It runs between 0.47x and 0.01x, well inside the sub-1.0x standard. That is not primarily operating efficiency. Burn multiple is a cash metric, and annual prepay suppresses cash burn, so the reading is flattered by float as much as by margin. It also becomes undefined from November 2029, when trailing free cash flow turns positive and there is no burn left to divide. Because it never exceeds 1.0x in this model, the burn multiple condition in the raise gate confirms rather than tests: the binding gate is crossing $100M ARR.

Agents is modelled as subscription-shaped, and it is not. Attach rate times ACV gives Agents a contracted ARR shape. A consumption product does not behave that way: revenue varies with usage month to month, there is no contracted floor to retain, and the right metrics are net revenue retention on consumed dollars and a usage ramp within each account rather than a logo attach rate. Billing in arrears is modelled, which is the part that matters most for cash. The rest is a deliberate simplification at this level and the first thing to build out properly.
